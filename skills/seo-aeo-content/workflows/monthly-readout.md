# Monthly SEO/AEO content readout

Use this workflow once a month or after a meaningful 28-day window. It converts observations into the next small backlog instead of rewarding volume for its own sake.

## Compare like with like

Record the current and prior windows, then separate:

- Google/Bing/other organic sessions from direct, referral, and ChatGPT referrals;
- Search Console impressions/clicks/CTR/position from GA4 sessions/engagement;
- article attention from `app_store_click` events;
- English and Vietnamese URLs;
- established pages from pages with too little data.

## Classify each important page

Use `keep`, `improve`, `new supporting page`, `consolidate`, `repair technical`, or `wait`. Include the evidence and confidence for each decision. A page with fewer than 10 sessions or 5 active users is normally `wait` unless it has a clear defect.

Look for patterns across the set:

- topics that earn reach but lose readers in the opening;
- topics that hold attention but are hard to discover;
- pages that get attention but no measured CTA click;
- internal-link hubs that do not pass readers to the next useful page;
- repeated 404s, alternate URLs, or stale pages;
- claims or sources that need a material refresh.

## Produce the next backlog

Limit the next cycle to:

- the highest-impact technical repair;
- one existing-article improvement;
- one new article only when the gap is distinct and supported;
- one measurement or instrumentation check.

For every item specify the owner, URL or slug, hypothesis, expected signal, files to touch, and review date. Do not claim an SEO/AEO win until the relevant Search Console or GA4 window has passed.
