# Cover Letters, LinkedIn Outreach, Deep Research & Offer-Stage Prep

This reference governs the four high-leverage conversion workflows after evaluating a role:
1. **Keyword-Mirrored Cover Letters (`cover`)**
2. **LinkedIn & Direct Persona Outreach (`contacto`)**
3. **6-Axis Deep Company Research (`deep`)**
4. **Offer-Stage Contract Reading Companion (`offer-prep`)**

---

## 1. Keyword-Mirrored Cover Letter Workflow (`cover`)

Never generate a generic, AI-cliché cover letter. Follow this sequence:

1. **Extract JD Keywords (Two Buckets):**
   - **ATS-Critical:** Exact role titles, frameworks, methodologies, and domain nouns.
   - **Human Trust Signals:** Action verbs and problem framing the team uses in the JD opening.
2. **Select 4–5 Verifiable Achievement Bullets from `cv.md`:**
   - Format each bullet as: `**Bold lead phrase,** one sentence of concrete impact with a metric from cv.md.`
   - Mirror the JD's vocabulary around the bullet without altering any factual metric, employer, or title.
3. **Strict Prose & Anti-Slop Rules:**
   - **350–420 words total** (excluding header/contact block).
   - **Active voice only** — zero passive phrasing (`"was delivered"`, `"were led"`).
   - **Zero em-dashes (`—`)** in cover letter prose.
   - **Banned filler & buzzwords:** Never use *"I am writing to express"*, *"I am excited/thrilled"*, *"passionate"*, *"holistic"*, *"synergy"*, *"move the needle"*, *"north star"*, *"perfect fit"*, or *"strong track record"*.
   - **Self-check:** If a sentence could appear in a cover letter for any other company, rewrite it with specific company/product context.
4. **Draft-in-Chat Approval Gate:** Always show the plain-text draft in chat and wait for user approval before rendering a PDF.

## 2. LinkedIn & Direct Outreach Engine (`contacto`)

Applications put you in a queue; targeted outreach gets you a conversation.

### Character Budget Rules
- **LinkedIn Free Tier Connection Note:** **<= 200 characters** (hard limit; default to 200 unless the user confirms LinkedIn Premium/Sales Navigator, which allows **<= 300 characters**).
- **Short Chat / Job-Board Opener (`Greeting` variant):** **<= 150 characters**.
- **Hard bans:** Never say *"I'm passionate about"*, never use corporate fluff, never include a phone number in a cold connection note.

### 3-Sentence Framework by Persona

| Target Persona | Sentence 1 | Sentence 2 | Sentence 3 (CTA) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Recruiter** | **Fit:** Role title + years/domain + location/availability | **Proof:** 1–2 hard screening metrics answered upfront | *"Happy to share my CV if this aligns with what you're looking for."* |
| **Hiring Manager** | **Hook:** Specific technical/product challenge their team is tackling | **Proof:** Your strongest quantified achievement solving that exact problem class | *"Would love to hear how your team is approaching [specific challenge]."* |
| **Team Peer (Referral)** | **Interest:** Specific reference to their talk, blog post, PR, or architecture | **Connection:** What you are building in the same technical space (not a job pitch) | *"Working on similar problems — would love your take on [topic]."* |
| **Interviewer (Pre-Call)** | **Research:** Specific nod to their background or recent team launch | **Context:** Brief link to your relevant experience | *"Looking forward to our conversation on [date]."* |

## 3. 6-Axis Deep Company Research (`deep`)

Before an interview or high-priority application, research and synthesize these 6 axes:
1. **Product & AI/Technical Strategy:** Core stack, architecture, engineering blog posts, open-source repos, and technical moat.
2. **Recent Moves (Last 6 Months):** Launches, funding rounds, acquisitions, key leadership/staff hires, or pivots.
3. **Engineering & Product Culture:** Shipping cadence, remote vs. office reality, org structure, and public developer feedback.
4. **Likely 6–12 Month Bottlenecks:** Scaling pain points, migrations, latency/cost challenges, or enterprise adoption hurdles implied by the JD.
5. **Competitive Landscape:** Top 2–3 competitors and where this company wins or trails.
6. **Candidate Angle:** Which 2 projects from `cv.md` to showcase and the exact narrative hook to lead with in the first 2 minutes of the interview.

## 4. Offer-Stage Contract Reading Companion (`offer-prep`)

When the candidate receives an offer letter or employment contract, act as a **structured reading companion** (never a lawyer, never outputting `"safe to sign"` or legal enforceability verdicts):

1. **Intake & Promises Check:** Record everything promised verbally or by email (base, bonus, equity, remote policy, title, start date, severance) and list any incorporated documents not yet provided (Option Plan, PIIA/IP assignment, Handbook).
2. **10-Point Clause Walk (Quote Verbatim, Describe in Plain English):**
   - Walk through: (1) Compensation & discretionary bonus, (2) Equity vesting/cliff/acceleration/exercise window, (3) Termination notice, cause definitions & severance, (4) Restrictive covenants (non-compete, non-solicit), (5) IP assignment & prior-work carve-outs, (6) Signing/relocation clawbacks, (7) Arbitration & forum, (8) Employee vs. contractor classification, (9) Working terms/PTO/location designation, (10) Entire-agreement integration clause.
   - Tag each notable clause descriptively: `[commonly negotiated]`, `[ask your lawyer]`, `[matches what you were told]`, `[differs from what you were told]`, or `[standard]`.
3. **Notable Absences & Consistency Deltas:** Explicitly list any promised term missing from the written contract (which the entire-agreement integration clause would otherwise erase).
4. **Two Actionable Lists:**
   - **Questions for Your Employment Lawyer** (jurisdiction-scoped and clause-anchored).
   - **Items to Raise with the Employer** (collaborative questions/topics for negotiation, plus an optional draft negotiation reply email that the user reviews and sends manually).
