# Petio UGC outreach workflow

Use this reference for creator discovery, UGC email campaigns, reply handling,
follow-ups, and learning from creator conversion data. The goal is a useful,
trustworthy creator conversation, not maximum send volume.

## Evidence-led starting point

Before changing the outreach approach, summarize the latest creator replies in a
small table. Separate product interest from offer fit and platform eligibility.
The recent Petio reply pattern supports these working hypotheses:

| Observed signal | Working interpretation | Workflow response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creators who connected Petio to a real pet problem asked for a brief or details | Specific owner context creates higher intent than a generic pitch | Open with one real, verified content detail and ask whether they want to try Petio |
| Several positive replies asked for price, attribution, deliverables, usage rights, or timing | Interest stalls when terms are vague | Prepare a complete brief; mark every unknown before sending |
| Many replies accepted only paid UGC | Rejection often means offer mismatch, not product rejection | Tag `paid-only`; do not re-pitch affiliate-only |
| Android-only creators could not use an iPhone-only product | Device fit is an eligibility gate | Segment before outreach and maintain an Android waitlist |
| A wrong-name email caused immediate trust damage | Identity errors are a hard quality failure | Verify the greeting against the exact recipient record immediately before send |
| No fixed timeline was acceptable to interested creators | Flexibility helps when stated plainly | Say there is no fixed delivery date; do not invent urgency |

These are Petio observations, not universal creator-marketing laws. Recheck them
against a comparable batch before changing the offer or prioritizing a content
type.

## Creator selection and diligence

Use only public, relevant creator signals and a legitimate public contact route.
Do not scrape private contact details, buy lists without provenance, or imply an
endorsement. Record the source URL, observed date, public audience/content fit,
pet-related reason for contact, platform/device signal, contact email, and any
paid-collaboration or opt-out signal.

Prioritize creators whose content naturally shows a pet routine, a real owner
problem, or a trusted day-in-the-life context. Fitness, gym, weight-loss, mom,
generic food, mukbang, and comedy categories are hypotheses only. Do not use a
large view count as a proxy for conversion fit. Record both the content pattern
and the intended Petio job.

Assign one status before drafting:

- `eligible-iphone`: creator can genuinely try the current iPhone app;
- `android-blocked`: creator is Android-only or cannot access the current app;
- `paid-only`: creator has clearly stated that affiliate-only or gifted work is
  not accepted;
- `unknown-fit`: device, contact permission, or commercial preference is not
  verified;
- `do-not-contact`: opt-out, prior negative experience, bounced address, or
  unsuitable contact route.

Do not send the same offer to every status. `android-blocked` and `paid-only`
are not normal prospects for the current affiliate-first campaign.

## Cohort routing

Assign both a status and a cohort before drafting. Do not compare response or
conversion rates across cohorts as if the offers were equivalent.

| Cohort | Include | First ask | Current action and hard stop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `organic-owner-affiliate` | Pet owners and day-in-the-life creators whose normal content already includes a real pet routine or problem | Invite them to try Petio, give honest feedback, and request the brief only if it feels useful | Use the approved affiliate offer. Do not imply a guaranteed fee or required endorsement. |
| `trusted-professional-trial` | Vets, trainers, educators, and other trusted pet professionals whose feedback could improve the product | Ask for product feedback first, with no content obligation | Offer complimentary Petio Plus testing access only when provisioning and trial length are confirmed. Do not ask for a post before they have evaluated the app. |
| `paid-ugc-parked` | Creators who state that they accept only fixed-fee work | Acknowledge their rate model and close politely | Do not send an affiliate-only counteroffer. Revisit only when a fixed budget exists. |
| `android-waitlist` | Relevant creators who cannot test an iPhone-only app | Ask permission to reconnect after Android launch, if appropriate | Do not pitch current collaboration or imply Android availability. |
| `unknown-fit-qualification` | Relevant creators whose device or commercial preference is not verified | Ask one short eligibility question | Do not send the full offer until device and offer fit are known. |

The cohort is determined by verified fit, not follower count. Large reach does
not override device, commercial, trust, or product relevance constraints.

## Offer and proof gates

The current approved offer is:

- no upfront creator fee is available right now;
- the creator receives a personal promo or affiliate link;
- the creator receives 50% of subscription revenue from referred customers;
- Petio is currently available on iPhone only.

The current app and landing-page links are:

- landing page: https://www.petiogo.com
- iPhone app: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/petio-ai-pet-care-health/id6745301343

Do not present the affiliate arrangement as paid UGC. Do not state the current
subscription price, attribution window, payout duration, usage rights, content
deliverables, deadline, or approval process until each term is verified in the
current offer brief. A possible future price such as $8.99 is not a current
offer term and must not appear as a confirmed fact.

The creator should be invited to try Petio and share honest feedback before
being asked to commit to a scripted deliverable. If the creator asks for a
brief, respond with the confirmed terms, not a promise to figure them out later.

## Creator brief release gate

Use [the creator brief template](../templates/creator-brief.md). Every field must
be labeled `CONFIRMED`, `UNCONFIRMED`, or `NOT APPLICABLE`, with a source and
verification date for confirmed commercial terms.

The brief is `brief-ready` only when these fields are confirmed or explicitly
not applicable:

- current subscription plan and price presented to the referred customer;
- audience promo or discount, including eligibility and expiry;
- what the 50% share is calculated from;
- attribution method and window;
- commission duration, payout frequency, method, currency, and minimum;
- refund, cancellation, tax, and chargeback treatment;
- requested deliverables, or an explicit statement that there is no content
  obligation during product evaluation;
- organic usage, paid-ad usage, whitelisting, editing, territory, and duration;
- revision and approval process;
- deadline, or an explicit statement that there is no fixed timeline;
- Petio Plus testing access, trial length, supported devices, and provisioning;
- creator disclosure requirements and support contact;
- current landing-page link, App Store link, and app screenshots.

If any compensation, attribution, rights, deliverable, or access field is
`UNCONFIRMED`, do not send a collaboration brief or request a commitment. A
short product-evaluation invitation is still allowed if it makes clear that no
content is required. Never use a possible future price, including $8.99, as the
current subscription price.

## Identity and copy preflight

Before sending each email, check all of the following:

1. Canonicalize the address to lowercase and compare it with the full Gmail
   history, including Sent, Inbox, Trash, and Spam where available.
2. Use the creator's exact signed name or verified profile name. If the name is
   uncertain, use `Hi there` rather than guessing.
3. Confirm the email is addressed to the intended creator, the profile evidence
   actually belongs to that creator, and the offer matches the creator status.
4. Include one concrete, true reason for reaching out. Remove it if it cannot
   be verified.
5. Include the app and landing-page links only when they help the creator
   evaluate the collaboration. The iPhone link is required for an iPhone-fit
   creator and must not be sent as if Android were supported.
6. Use one clear question or CTA. Keep the note short enough to sound like a
   person wrote it for that creator.
7. Do not use the em dash character in any outbound copy. Avoid generic
   phrases such as `I hope this finds you well`, `I came across your profile`,
   `leverage`, `synergy`, `excited to announce`, and feature-list language.
8. Include a simple opt-out path for cold outreach when appropriate. Honor an
   opt-out immediately and never follow up after it.

Hard stop if the name, recipient, platform fit, offer terms, link, or contact
permission is ambiguous. A smaller checked batch is preferable to a larger
batch with identity or eligibility mistakes.

## Base email shape

Use this as a structure, not a mail merge paragraph:

```text
Subject: A Petio idea for your content

Hi [verified name or there],

[One specific observation about their pet or content.] I’m building Petio, a
pet-care app, and I thought it might fit the way you already talk about [real
topic].

It’s currently iPhone-only. If you’re open to trying it, I’d value your honest
feedback first. If it feels useful and you want to make a short, natural video,
we can set up a personal promo link. We do not have an upfront creator budget
right now, but the offer is 50% of subscription revenue from customers you
refer.

Petio: https://www.petiogo.com
iPhone app: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/petio-ai-pet-care-health/id6745301343

Would you be open to trying it and sharing your honest feedback?

Thanks,
James
Petio
DocLap Software
```

Do not claim that the creator has used Petio, that the app solved a problem, or
that a video will convert. Do not attach a screenshot by default. A link lets
the creator choose to inspect the product; include a verified screenshot only
when it adds clarity and its source and current state are recorded.

detail, product fit, iPhone eligibility, feedback-first invitation, transparent
offer, one CTA.

## Reply classification and scripts

Classify every creator reply before drafting a response:

`high-intent`, `info-request`, `paid-only`, `android-blocked`, `unknown-fit`,
`wrong-name-or-trust`, `opt-out`, `not-now`, or `no-response`.

For `high-intent` or `info-request`, answer the creator's exact question first.
If the brief is `brief-ready`, include or attach the complete brief in the same
reply. Do not ask whether they want information they already requested:

```text
Hi [verified name],

Thanks, I’m glad Petio feels relevant for you and [pet name or real detail].
There’s no fixed delivery date on our side. I’d rather you try it and share
honest feedback than make something that does not feel natural.

I’ve included the brief with the subscription, attribution, deliverable, usage,
and payment terms so you can decide with the full picture. Here are the product
links as well:

Petio: https://www.petiogo.com
iPhone app: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/petio-ai-pet-care-health/id6745301343

After you’ve had a look, tell me what you think or send over any questions.

Thanks,
James
```

If the brief is `blocked`, do not imply that complete terms are available:

```text
Hi [verified name],

Thanks for asking. We’re still finalizing the attribution, payout, and usage
terms, so I do not want to ask you to agree to an incomplete offer.

If you’re still interested, you’re welcome to try Petio with no content
obligation and tell us what feels useful, confusing, or missing. I’ll send the
full brief once those terms are confirmed.

Petio: https://www.petiogo.com
iPhone app: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/petio-ai-pet-care-health/id6745301343

Thanks,
James
```

For `android-blocked`:

```text
Hi [verified name],

Thanks for letting me know. Petio is currently iPhone-only, so it would not be
fair to ask you to create around it right now. I’ll keep your contact for when
Android is available. I appreciate you taking the time.

Thanks,
James
```

For `paid-only`:

```text
Hi [verified name],

Thanks for being clear. We do not have a fixed creator fee available yet, so I
won’t try to push you into an affiliate arrangement. I’ll keep you in mind if a
paid budget opens up.

Thanks,
James
```

For `wrong-name-or-trust`, apologize once, use the correct name only after
verification, and do not add a sales pitch:

```text
Hi [verified name],

I’m sorry I used the wrong name in my earlier email. That was my mistake. I
understand if you would rather not continue, and I’ll be more careful.

Thanks,
James
```

If a creator asks about an unconfirmed term, say it is being finalized and do
not request a commitment until the written brief is complete. If the product
is not a fit, close the loop politely instead of trying to overcome the
objection.

## Follow-up sequence

Follow the creator's state, not a blind volume schedule:

| Timing | When to use | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Same day or next business day | Positive reply or question | Answer the exact question and provide the brief or next step |
| 3 to 4 days | No response after the initial email | One personal nudge with a different sentence and an easy decline |
| 8 to 10 days | Still no response | Final low-pressure close; stop afterward |
| Any time | Opt-out, paid-only, Android blocker, or clear rejection | Do not continue the campaign sequence |

Non-response nudge:

```text
Hi [verified name],

Just checking in in case my note got buried. I thought of you because of [specific
pet or content detail]. Petio is iPhone-only, and we’re looking for honest
feedback from people who might actually use it. There’s no fixed deadline.

If you want the brief, reply `brief`. If it is not relevant, no reply is needed.

Thanks,
James
```

Never send a follow-up that changes the offer, repeats the same generic copy, or
pretends urgency. Record the next follow-up date and the stop reason.

## Experiment design and send gate

Run outreach as small, labeled cohorts of 10 to 15 creators. In each test,
change one variable only, such as creator type, personalized hook, first CTA, or
feedback-first framing. Hold the offer, send window, links, eligibility rules,
and follow-up timing constant. Do not mix owner-creators and paid UGC
professionals in one experiment cell.

Use positive reply rate or brief-request rate as the primary early metric.
Guardrails are wrong-name incidents, bounces, opt-outs, paid-only mismatch, and
Android mismatch. Installs, activation, referred subscriptions, and revenue are
the later conversion metrics.

Label each cohort observation:

- `early`: less than 24 hours since the last initial email;
- `directional`: 24 to 72 hours since the last initial email;
- `stable`: at least 72 hours with follow-up state recorded;
- `promotable-pattern`: at least three comparable stable observations with a
  consistent downstream result.

Do not draw conclusions or launch another broad batch from an `early` cohort.
Normally wait until the previous cohort is at least `directional`. If the user
explicitly prioritizes volume, keep later cohorts small and separately labeled
so the test remains interpretable.

## Gmail deduplication and send controls

For any batch request:

1. Export or search Gmail-wide history with `in:anywhere`; canonicalize every
   address and collect Sent, Inbox, Trash, Spam, drafts, bounces, opt-outs, and
   prior apology threads where visible.
2. Build a candidate ledger with email, verified name, source URL, device,
   content pattern, status, first-contact date, last-contact date, thread ID,
   offer variant, and next action.
3. Exclude every previously contacted address, even if the earlier message was
   a draft, bounced, apologized for, or sent under a mistaken name. A new
   campaign requires a new verified address, not a new subject line.
4. Draft a small batch. Before each send, inspect the recipient chip, greeting,
   personalization, links, offer, and opt-out wording in the actual composer.
5. Send only after the preflight passes. After each small batch, reconcile Sent
   with the ledger. If the browser times out or the state is unclear, stop and
   inspect Sent before retrying. Never resend blindly.
6. Report attempted, sent, failed, duplicate-excluded, and unresolved counts
   separately. Do not call a draft or open composer a sent email.

## Measurement and learning loop

Track the funnel by creator and content pattern:

`researched` → `contacted` → `delivered` → `replied` → `positive` →
`brief-requested` → `iPhone-eligible` → `installed` → `activated` →
`promo-click` → `referred-subscriber` → `subscription-revenue`.

Also record `paid-only`, `android-blocked`, `opt-out`, `wrong-name`, `no-response`,
and `unsubscribed` outcomes. Review rates by comparable cohorts such as creator
type, day-in-the-life versus generic food/comedy, pet problem, trust vehicle,
device, offer variant, and follow-up stage.

Separate:

- **attention:** views, reach, opens, and watch time;
- **conversation:** replies, positive intent, brief requests, and qualified
  questions;
- **conversion:** verified clicks, installs, activation, referred subscribers,
  and revenue.

High views with weak clicks, installs, or referred subscribers is
`intent-poor`, not a winner. Promote a pattern only after at least three
comparable observations or a clearly documented directional result. Keep the
creator, topic, timing, device, offer, and distribution confounders visible.

## Creator landing-page gate

Do not publish a creator landing page while core commercial or usage terms are
unconfirmed. Once the brief is `brief-ready`, the page should contain a short
human introduction, who the collaboration fits, the iPhone-only limitation, a
feedback-first invitation, the exact offer and terms, current app screenshots,
the landing-page and App Store links, a concise FAQ, disclosure and usage-rights
language, and a real support contact.

The page must not contain `UNCONFIRMED` placeholders or present a future price
as current. Record the page URL and screenshot date in the creator ledger so
outreach does not link to stale terms or product images.
