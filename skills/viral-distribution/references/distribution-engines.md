# Distribution Engines Reference: Clipping, UGC & Slideshows

Organic social distribution on short-form algorithmic platforms (TikTok,
Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) relies on three legitimate, non-interchangeable
distribution mechanisms. Operating without understanding their differences
leads to capital waste or low-intent vanity views.

Phone farms, account fleets, and multi-account hardware coordination are out
of scope for this skill: they violate platform terms of service. See the
Out-of-Scope Boundaries in SKILL.md.

## 2. Engine Deep Dives

### Engine A: Clipping Campaigns
- **Definition:** Turning long-form or high-production source assets (podcasts, livestreams, film cuts, keynote demos, gameplay) into short-form cut-downs distributed via decentralized third-party accounts.
- **Where clippers live:**
  - Dedicated agency Discord servers (vetted pools of 1,000 to 100,000+ clippers).
  - Open creator marketplaces (e.g., Whop, bounties).
- **Economic Model:**
  - Pure performance CPM. You set a budget cap (e.g., $2,500) and target CPM ($0.50 to $1.50).
  - Clippers submit live post links once milestones (e.g. 10k, 50k, 100k views) are reached.
  - Zero upfront creation fee. You pay only for verified view delivery.
- **Risks & Vulnerabilities:**
  - **Botting & View Fraud:** On unmanaged platforms (e.g., open Whop boards), clippers frequently buy SMM panel views to drain campaign pools.
  - **Geo Dilution:** Millions of views originating from non-target or Tier-3 regions where advertising value and purchasing power are zero.
  - **Negative Brand Association:** Unvetted clippers adding sensationalist or misleading clickbait captions that hurt brand equity.
- **Best Suited For:**
  - Entertainment, streaming series, film & TV launches (Apple TV+, Amazon Prime models).
  - High-profile founders/podcasts with hours of existing spoken-word footage.
  - Gaming launches with cinematic or hype moments.

### Engine B: UGC (User-Generated Content) Programs
- **Definition:** Contracting individual human creators to record authentic, casual, problem-solution videos. Distinct from traditional *influencer marketing*: UGC is posted to *fresh or dedicated brand accounts*, relying on algorithmic FYP distribution rather than the creator's existing follower count.
- **Economic Model:**
  - Hybrid compensation: Fixed Base Fee + Performance CPM (e.g., $20–$50 flat creation fee + $1.00–$2.00 CPM on views over 10k).
  - Most expensive per-creative upfront, but highest customer acquisition efficiency.
- **Operational Reality:**
  - High friction. Once a winning hook or script angle is discovered, the operator must immediately pass that winning script across the entire roster of creators.
  - High creator churn: Competitors will poach reliable creators with higher base fees.
  - Strict pre-publication QA: "Submit video draft for approval before publishing, or forfeit payment."
- **Rejection of Synthetic AI Avatars:**
  - Synthetic talking-head avatars (HeyGen, Hedra, Higgsfield) are rejected as AI slop: audiences and platform moderation algorithms quickly identify and downrank synthetic talking heads, resulting in viewer distrust and account penalties.
  - Genuine creator faces and hands demonstrating real product workflows yield the highest sustained conversion.
- **Best Suited For:**
  - Consumer mobile applications (dating, fitness, productivity, utilities).
  - E-commerce / DTC products requiring visual demonstrations or before-and-after proof.
  - Any product where purchase intent requires trust and human identification.

### Engine C: Slideshow Syndication Programs (TikTok/IG Photo Carousels)
- **Definition:** Distributing curated photo-mode carousels (6–10 slides) across a syndication network of independent, aged creator accounts recruited from creator Discord communities.
- **Why It Works:**
  - **High Consumption & Low Friction:** Users swipe at their own pace; effortless consumption drives completion rate and sends powerful retention signals to algorithmic feeds.
  - **Save & Bookmark Magnet:** Carousel checklists, routine diagnostics, comparisons, and mental models trigger bookmarking rates 3x–5x higher than standard video. Saves directly drive secondary algorithmic amplification.
  - **Zero Filming Overhead:** No actors, cameras, lighting, or vocal tracks required. Decks are designed cleanly as high-density visual cards.
- **Economic Model (Milestone Hurdles):**
  - **0 – 100k views:** $0 payout. Algorithmic flops cost the brand nothing.
  - **100k views milestone:** $100 payout ($1.00 effective CPM).
  - **1M views milestone:** $350 payout ($0.35 effective CPM).
  - **2M views milestone:** Capped at ~$500 ($0.25 effective CPM).
  - **Loyalty bonuses:** Surprise $25–$50 bonuses sent on hit posts to secure creator loyalty and daily posting discipline.
- **Creator Recruitment & Sourcing:**
  - Source native creators with existing, active TikTok accounts in target geographies (US, UK, CA, AU) via public creator Discord servers (editor and clipping communities).
  - Pitch: transparent performance payout with guaranteed payouts upon hitting public view milestones.
  - Avoid proxies, VPNs, or device fleets: syndicating through real human creators on real native accounts ensures genuine regional FYP targeting without platform bans.
- **The Distribution-First Product Playbook:**
  - **Step 1 — Trend Validation:** Before building or scoping features, inspect TikTok Search Insights for exploding search queries (+100% to +200% weekly growth) in targeted sub-niches (e.g. Wellness → Women → Relationship Anxiety).
  - **Step 2 — Format Autopsy:** Identify accounts posting daily with >=100k views in the last 30 days. Prioritize unmonetized "normie" accounts posting for fun with zero products attached.
  - **Step 3 — Feature Mirroring:** Build the app around the format's core stopping power. The hero feature of the app must directly deliver the utility promised in the slide hook.
- **Best Suited For:**
  - B2C utility and habit mobile apps (fitness checklists, relationship tools, mental health trackers, routine organizers).
  - Micro-SaaS tools with visual outputs (resumes, prompts, design templates).
  - Info-products and Notion systems transitioning into mobile software.

## 3. Scaling a Winner Without Evading Anything

When one hook, script angle, or carousel format wins, the legitimate moves are:

1. **Script & Deck dispersion:** hand the winning script or slide format to independent creators — each creator posts natively with localized audio pairings.
2. **Moment dispersion:** give clippers different source moments from the same long-form asset; distinct cuts are distinct content.
3. **Format iteration:** vary the hook slide, visual palette, and caption framing per creator rather than mandating one identical re-upload.
4. **Paid amplification:** when organic replication is exhausted and more reach is needed, the answer is paid ads — not account games.

If someone asks to repost the identical file from many accounts, or to
re-render it with pixel-hash tricks (fake screen frames, background swaps)
specifically to defeat duplicate detection — refuse. That is duplicate-content
evasion and it is out of scope for this skill.
